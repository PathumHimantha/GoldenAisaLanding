Our Story

Empowering Communities Since 2010

Golden Asia Micro Credit Finance was founded with a vision to provide accessible financial solutions to individuals and small businesses across Sri Lanka. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a trusted financial partner with multiple branches island-wide, offering micro credit loans, leasing options, and personalized financial services.

In 2025, we proudly introduced our own Loan Management System, a digital platform designed to simplify loan processing, improve transparency, and support our clients with real-time financial insights. This step reflects our commitment to innovation and community growth.

What types of loans do you provide?

We provide daily loans, business loans, and finance leasing solutions to meet both personal and business needs. Each loan package is tailored with flexible repayment options to suit your requirements.

How can I apply for a loan?

You can apply by visiting our office at Annasigala, Molagoda, Kegalle, or contact us through phone/email. Our team will guide you through the application process and required documentation.

What documents are required?

Typically, you’ll need a valid NIC, proof of address (utility bill or similar), and proof of income (salary slip or business records). Additional documents may be required based on the type of loan.

How soon can I get my loan approved?

Loan approval times vary depending on the loan type and documents provided. In most cases, approvals are completed within 24 to 48 hours.

Do you offer flexible repayment options?

Yes, we provide flexible repayment schedules such as daily, weekly, or monthly plans, depending on your income and financial situation.

Can't find the answer to your question?

Our support team is here to help. If your question isn’t listed here, feel free to contact us directly via phone, email, or our online form. We’ll be glad to guide you.
